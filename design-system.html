Design system

"Modern newsroom" — NYT × Instagram: crisp white, hairline grid, image-first cards, pill actions, blur-glass masthead. Editorial serif authority kept (Fraunces); navy & gold demoted to accents; red reserved exclusively for giving.

Rules: red appears nowhere except giving actions. Gold text on white uses #8A6C10 (4.5:1+); bright gold #C9A227 lives on navy. Hairlines #E8E7EE. Media radius 12px, cards 18px, actions are pills. All pairs contrast-checked.

Typography

Fraunces 600 — display & headlines
Fraunces italic — pull quotes, chapter numerals
Archivo 400/500 — body and interface text, 16px minimum
Archivo 700 · letterspaced caps — kickers & labels

Two families, three weights each — nothing else ships.

Motion & accessibility policy

  • Content is visible immediately — motion is an accent, never a gate. The hero's one entrance animation runs only under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.
  • WCAG AA contrast on every text/background pair; one H1 per page; alt text on all imagery; skip link; keyboard focus visible everywhere.
  • Semantic HTML throughout — headings, nav, main, footer — so search engines and AI assistants can read the site's structure (the GEO baseline).
