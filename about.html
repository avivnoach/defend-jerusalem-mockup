About us

Safeguard human rights. Fight intolerance. Defend Jerusalem.

We lead the legal struggle to safeguard Jerusalem — to ensure the city's unity, its civil rights, and the Jewish people's right to live and worship there freely, so that its community can never be exiled again.

Why we exist

After two millennia, Jerusalem has returned to Jewish sovereignty — and a determined opposition refuses to accept it. That opposition is driven not only by political motives, but by intolerance toward the biblical and historical realities of the Jewish connection to the city.

For years, intolerance, antisemitism, and extremist violence have endangered Jerusalem. Its destruction has been threatened by political forces, by terrorists, and by neighboring governments — in courtrooms, in international forums, and on the street.

We are fighting back — not with slogans, but the way lawyers fight: with cases, with evidence, and with daylight.

The work

Problem, solution, approach

I

The problem

Intolerance, antisemitism, and extremist violence endanger Jerusalem — its unity threatened by political pressure abroad and incitement at home.

II

The solution

The civil rights of the city's residents, and sovereignty over an undivided Jerusalem, must be protected and upheld — in law, not only in words.

III

Our approach

An activist legal campaign waged in countries around the world: filings, investigations, and published research defending the right to live and worship freely in Jerusalem.

Director's message

“The fight for Jerusalem is not just about preserving a city — it's about standing up for the values of justice and freedom for all.”

In 1967, after two thousand years, Jerusalem was reunified — and for the first time in centuries, every one of its holy sites stood open to every faith. That is the city we defend: not a possession, but a promise kept.

Since that day the city has remained under relentless assault — not only by violence, but by propaganda and international pressure that would strip it of its history and place it under a rule that has never offered such openness. What terror could not accomplish, its sponsors now pursue through campaigns and coercion.

So we stand our ground, case by case: that the city remain open, that every holy site be protected, that everyone within its walls worship without fear — Jerusalem, the reunified capital, a beacon of freedom.

— The Office of the Director · Defend Jerusalem

What we stand for

Four commitments

iCivil rights for the whole city

Tolerance and equal protection for every community that calls Jerusalem home.

iiThe bond to the capital, defended in law

The Jewish people's connection to Jerusalem is historical fact — and we defend it as legal fact.

iiiEvery holy site open

Every faith free to worship at its holy places, without fear — as only a united city has ensured.

ivEvidence over slogans

We document, we litigate, and we publish — so that anyone can verify what we claim.

Stand with the city.

The legal defense of Jerusalem runs on the people who show up for it — with a gift, or simply by reading and sharing the work.
